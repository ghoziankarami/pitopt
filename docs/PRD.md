# PitOpt public scope

PitOpt is a local, single-user strategic open-pit planning prototype.
Inputs are an axis-aligned block model, topography and explicit assumptions.
Outputs include maximum-closure shells, shell ranking, heuristic pushbacks,
heuristic schedules, bench geometry, reconciliation and warnings.

Only generated tin and porphyry examples are included. No private client
models, economic results or project-specific assumptions are public fixtures.

The maximum-closure objective is optimal for the supplied discrete graph.
Schedules, pit-shell ranking, slope discretisation, ramp construction and
geotechnical interpretation are separate approximations. The output is not
an approved reserve estimate, feasibility study or construction-ready design.

See METODOLOGI.md and RELEASE_AUDIT.md for evidence and limitations.
